The bracket is designed to securely straddle a house brick, longways, with adjustment between 205 to 240mm, with the ratchet moving in 2mm increments. The front hook, drops 20mm, the rear 50mm. The rear hook allows for a rear safety anchor, as required. The ratchet has a paracord lock; just in case. If the sizing is finessed, the lock isn't required.

## Files
- **clip_main_240.stl** is the front 20 mm leg plus the top bar. The light hook sits at the bottom of the leg and opens upward, so you can press the cable down and in, and it clicks through the 2.3 mm opening. The top bar is a lightweight lattice so its stiff; resting on three small feet. Both legs are angled inward slightly and have small grip ridges.

- **clip_back_leg_slider.stl** is the 50 mm back leg. It slides onto the end of the bar and ratchets in 2 mm steps; the inside gap can be between and  240 mm. Push the leg on firmly against a wall, and it clicks tight; lift the thumb tab to release.
- The paracord lock: press a section of paracord down into the pocket above the ratchet catch, beside the thumb tab. The cord fills the space the catch needs to lift into, so the leg can't loosen while the cord is in. Push it right in until it drops into the pocket, because the lock only works there.
Anchor tie point: the foot of the back leg has a push-in slot for tying to each anchor point. It opens upward, so pulling down on the cord seats it deeper.

- **test_clip_short.stl** has the same leg, hook and slider mechanism as the main clip, but a short bar. It adjusts from 67 to 110 mm, so it can clamp a loose brick across its 102.5 mm width.

## Print Settings
- Test printed on Bambu P1S in Bambu PETG.  
- 3hr(ish) print.  
- Use 4 walls.  
- Use 25% gyroid infill for the main clip, and 100% for the slider.  
- Each clip uses about 30–45 g of PETG, depending on infill.  
- The slider starts 3 mm below the main clip's base level. Bambu Studio drops it onto the bed automatically.  

## Key dimensions
- Inside gap between legs: 205–240 mm (ratchet, 2 mm pitch, 1.2 mm teeth)
- Both legs toe in (~2.5–2.9°) with flared tips and 0.5 mm grip ridges
- Feet: front foot, mid foot, slider floor (3-point contact)
- Light hook: Ø5.2 mm cradle, 2.3 mm snap opening, opens upward
- Paracord lock pocket: 4.4 mm tall over the pawl; the cord in the pocket stops the pawl lifting
- Rail 10 mm deep, slider tunnel clearance 0.3 mm per side
- Approx. material per clip (main + slider) at 100% infill: ~36 cm³ ≈ 45 g PETG
